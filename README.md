# 💫 About Me:
Hi, I’m Luís Antolin, a Data Scientist with a strong passion for turning complex data into actionable insights. My work sits at the intersection of machine learning, risk modeling, and AI, with hands-on experience in industries like (re)insurance, agriculture and quantitative finances.<br>

# 📚 Scientific Publications
I’ve had the opportunity to contribute to impactful research during MSc and PhD academic years. Below are a few selected publications that reflect my work using data science to generate usefull information on sustainable food systems, yield modeling, and climate change adaptation:

[How much would irrigation increase maize production in Brazil?](https://www.scielo.br/j/sa/a/8tnfCgF5QzNtQ5zy86tgpyg/)
Luis Antolin et al. (2025), Scientia Agricola

[Impact assessment of common bean availability in Brazil under climate change scenarios](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0308521X2100127X) Luis Antolin et al. (2021), Agricultural Systems

[Protecting the Amazon forest and reducing global warming via agricultural intensification](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41893-022-00968-8) Marin et al. (2022), Nature Sustainability


These works focus on modeling strategies to close yield gaps, improve resource use efficiency, and support decision-making in agricultural planning under future climate scenarios.
